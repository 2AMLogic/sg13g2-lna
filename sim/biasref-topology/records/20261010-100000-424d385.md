# Record 20261010-100000-424d385

- **Experiment**: biasref-topology -- **correction record** (no new
  simulation; issue
  [#149](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-lna/issues/149)). Source
  records `20260921-160018-a46ed37` and `20260921-173323-2aeafef` are
  untouched (append-only, `sim/README.md`); a correction mints a new record
  id, it never edits an older one.
- **What was wrong**: the `*-core-minigrid.csv` file of both source records
  declares a 10-column header
  (`...,vbref_v,vna_v,vsdb_v`) but every one of its 27 data rows has only 9
  fields: the `vsdb_v` value is missing. The cause was the embedded awk in
  `run_biasref_sweep.sh` (as committed at the time): its `printf` format had
  nine `%s` conversions for ten arguments, so the tenth (`kv["vsdb"]`) was
  silently dropped. The value is present in every retained log
  (`corners/<id>/core_*.log`, `BIASREF ... vsdb <v>`), so nothing was lost
  as evidence, only from the CSV.
- **What is NOT affected**: all other columns, including `iq3_a`, `iqa_a`,
  `vbref_v`, `vna_v`, every verdict, and the other four phase CSVs. A
  replay of all committed phase CSVs of both records from their retained
  logs with `reduce_biasref.py` is byte-identical for mpa-diode,
  core-startup, servo-minigrid and servo-startup, and byte-identical for
  core-minigrid after dropping the trailing `vsdb_v` column. No headline
  number quoted by either record or DR-0003 depends on `vsdb_v`.
- **Correction**: `20261010-100000-424d385-a46ed37-core-minigrid-corrected.csv`
  and `20261010-100000-424d385-2aeafef-core-minigrid-corrected.csv` are the
  full 10-column core mini-grid tables regenerated by
  `reduce_biasref.py` from the retained logs of the respective source
  record (Stage-1 `--phases stage1`, the other `--phases all`). The two
  files are identical, as the source record's note says Phases A-C
  reproduce the earlier record.
- **Guard**: `tests/test_reduce_biasref.py` asserts the committed
  core-minigrid CSVs equal the reducer output minus `vsdb_v`, asserts the
  corrected CSVs equal the reducer output exactly, and fails if any further
  historical discrepancy appears.
