# Record 20260921-124900-d6da30a

- **Experiment**: hbt-characterization — **correction record** (no new
  simulation). The `-40 C` and `125 C` NF cells of records
  `20260910-200059-7da7038` and `20260918-203652-4293920`, algebraically
  re-referenced to the fixed source temperature `T0 = 300.15 K` those
  records *claim* to use but do not (issue
  [#25](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-lna/issues/25)). Both source
  records are untouched (append-only, `sim/README.md`): a correction
  mints a new record id, it never edits an older one.
- **What was wrong**: ngspice-46 computes a resistor's **thermal-noise
  source at the analysis temperature** (`.options temp`) regardless of a
  per-instance `temp=` override — the override only reaches the
  resistance value's temperature coefficients. The bench's `Rs=50` source
  resistor therefore contributed noise at the swept **ambient** corner
  temperature (233.15 K / 300.15 K / 398.15 K), not at the fixed
  300.15 K the records' NF formula divides by. Measured, not assumed:
  `sim/lna-characterization/testbench/tb_resistor_noise_temp_probe.spice`
  (issue #18 / PR #28) reads `T_eff = 327.15 K` for *both* a
  `temp=27`-overridden and a bare 50 Ohm resistor at `.options temp=54`,
  and `sim/lna-characterization/README.md` ("Noise figure") documents the
  same convention gap on an independent bench.
- **Correction method** (exact, no fitting; see
  `rederive_nf_fixed_t0.py`, which regenerates every number in this
  record deterministically from the two committed CSVs — no PDK, no
  ngspice): noise powers from uncorrelated sources add, so the committed
  input-referred density decomposes as
  `inoise_committed^2 = N_rest + 4*k*T_amb*Rs` with
  `T_amb = temp_c + 273.15`, `Rs = 50 Ohm`, `k = 1.380649e-23 J/K`; the
  T0-referenced density is then
  `inoise_T0^2 = 10^(NF_committed/10) * 4*k*T0*Rs + 4*k*Rs*(T0 - T_amb)`
  and `NF_T0 = 10*log10(inoise_T0^2 / (4*k*T0*Rs))`. `N_rest` (HBT, the
  deliberately-unpinned `RL`, bias tees) is unchanged — only the source
  resistor's temperature is corrected, which is the only thing the
  records' own claim was false about. `27 C` cells are identity
  (`T_amb = T0`): they were already correct as documented and are
  deliberately **not** duplicated here — the source records remain the
  citation for them.
- **Affected cells**: every `-40 C` and `125 C` cell of both records —
  3638 data rows, 140 cells (20260910: 40 `Nx=1` cells;
  20260918: 100 cells across `Nx={1,8}`). `gain_db`, `ft_hz` and the bias
  columns are copied verbatim — a source-temperature correction cannot
  change them; only `nf_db` is re-derived.
- **Resolution limit**: the source records commit `nf_db` to 6 decimals;
  the recovered density is exact modulo that quantization (<2e-7 dB per
  point). The correction itself ranges from ~2e-6 dB on far-subthreshold
  rows (the source term is negligible there at NF≈56 dB — the correction
  is below the data's own resolution, stated per row in
  `nf_delta_db`) to **-1.009 dB** at the `Nx=8` `bcs`/125 C optimum.
  A round-trip identity (re-referencing back to `T_amb` must reproduce
  the committed NF) holds to 7.1e-15 dB on every row.
- **Validity flags**: `20260918`'s committed `validity_flags` column is
  reproduced **exactly** (0 mismatches in 3898 rows) by the same three
  model-card limits its README documents, which license classifying
  `20260910`'s rows (no such column) after the fact — the derivation
  asserts this match before emitting anything.
- **Headline corrected figures** (record `20260918-203652-4293920`;

  optimum and binding-corner values are the same numbers below):

  - Noise optimum, whole grid, in-box, `Nx=1`: corrected **5.363 dB**

  - Noise optimum, whole grid, `Nx=8` (all points **and** in-box —
    same row): corrected **0.963 dB** (was 1.972, `-1.009`) at
    `bcs`/125 C, `V_CE`=0.6 V, `J_C`=22.12 mA/um^2. **The whole-grid
    best-NF cell moves corner**: under a fixed-T0 reference the hot
    corner wins, not `bcs`/-40 C.
  - Binding corner per `target-spec.md` (`wcs`/125 C), `Nx=1`:
    corrected **8.309 dB** (was 8.513, `-0.204`) unrestricted;
    **8.534 dB** (was 8.729, `-0.194`) in-box.
  - Binding corner (`wcs`/125 C), `Nx=8`: corrected **2.047 dB**
    (was 2.853, `-0.805`), in-box and unrestricted (same row).
  - **Worst cell after correction**: `Nx=1` still `wcs`/125 C
    (**8.634 dB** at `V_CE`=0.6 V for both scopes, was 8.824) — the
    `Nx=1` binding corner statement is unchanged. **`Nx=8` flips**:
    its worst corrected cell is now `wcs`/**-40 C** (**2.484 dB**
    all-points, **2.497 dB** in-box, was 1.899/1.914) — under a
    fixed-T0 reference the *cold* corner is `Nx=8`'s binding NF
    corner, not the hot one.
  - `Nx=8`-vs-`Nx=1` in-box advantage at `V_CE`=0.8 V, corrected:
    **3.5-6.5 dB at every corner/temp** (worst `bcs`/-40 C 3.52, widest
    `wcs`/125 C 6.49; was 3.9-5.9 committed) — strengthened, not
    weakened, by the correction.
  - `V_CE` flatness of the corrected noise optimum over the full
    0.6-1.4 V range (affected temps): worst **0.400 dB** at `Nx=1`
    (`typ`/125 C and its `sf`/`fs` aliases), worst **0.084 dB** at
    `Nx=8` — both directionally the committed record's findings
    (0.378/0.066 dB): `V_CE` remains a weak, `Nx`-dependent second-order
    lever.
- **Impact on conclusions resting on the source records**: every
  qualitative conclusion survives — `Nx=8` beats `Nx=1` on NF at every
  corner by more than the committed margin, `V_CE`-flatness arguments
  hold, and `DR-0001`'s recommended bias remains in-box at every
  corner — but two *quantitative* statements change and should be cited
  from this record from now on: (1) the corrected `Nx=8` worst-in-grid NF
  is 2.50 dB, cold-corner-bound, not 2.86 dB hot-corner-bound; (2)
  hot-corner `Nx=8` corrected NF (~2.0 dB at `wcs`/125 C) is lower than
  the committed 2.85 dB. `spec/` is not touched by this correction
  (decision records are append-only; nothing here contradicts DR-0001's
  recommendation).
- **Bench unchanged going forward**: this record does not adopt a new
  testbench — it re-references committed data. Future NF benches in
  this tree should use the `noisy=0` + analytic-source-term shape
