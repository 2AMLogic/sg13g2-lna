# Erratum to record `20261106-054735-64b37ae`: 125 C temperature slice

This is a correction record (issue #306). It withdraws the unqualified
full-grid reading of [`20261106-054735-64b37ae.md`](20261106-054735-64b37ae.md),
which stays unchanged as first-pass evidence: `sim/` is append-only, so a
correction mints a new `<record-id>` (`sim/README.md`, "Append-only rule").
No line of the corrected record has been modified, moved or removed.

## What that record actually covers

`20261106-054735-64b37ae` was generated with `LNA_TEMPS=125` (a temperature
slice), so its coverage check ran over the 15-cell, 125 C-only slice of the
45-cell PVT grid — not the full box. Its own text names neither the slice nor
the partial grid, so two of its lines overstate it when read unqualified:

- The **Claim** ("... at the 45 PVT cells ..."): every cell in that record is
  at 125 C (see `(15 cells)` in its headlines and `125C` in each worst-cell
  identity). No -40 C or 27 C cell was measured.
- "**Reduction**: ok (expected grid complete, all measurements finite)": this
  meant the *requested slice* was complete; it is NOT a full-grid completeness
  claim.

Record `20261106-054735-64b37ae` is therefore NOT a full 45-cell PVT campaign
result and must not be cited as one. Cite it only as the 125 C slice of the
issue-#258 `xout`-bleed campaign; the -40 C / 27 C cells of the same campaign
are carried by `20261106-055142-64b37ae`, `20261106-055453-64b37ae`,
`20261116-022647-eacd53a` and `20261116-023212-eacd53a` (see
`issue-258-xout-bleed-convergence-20261116.md`).
