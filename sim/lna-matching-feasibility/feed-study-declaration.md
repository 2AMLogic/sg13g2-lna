# Feed-study declaration (issue #193)

Committed BEFORE any simulation of this study. Generated by
`python3 -I sim/lna-matching-feasibility/feed_study.py declare`; a unit test
checks that this file equals that output, so it cannot drift from the code.

**NOMINAL-CELL EXPLORATION.** One PVT cell (hbt_typ / mos_tt, 27 C, 1.80 V). No
ratified-row or final-stability claim; `design/` and `spec/` are not modified.

## Question

Can a finite, model-backed parallel-LC tank in series with R3b (330 Ohm, DR-0003
reference retained, bias NOT retuned) recover a useful part of the noise
headroom that the ideal 1 uH choke exposes (NFmin@290 2.445 -> 0.467 dB in the
#186 what-if), on the unresized DUT?

## Candidate set

| Variant | Role | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `committed` | baseline | committed feed: R3b = 330 Ohm from bref to b1, unmodified |
| `ideal_choke` | diagnostic bound | ideal 1 uH choke in series with R3b (the #186 what-if; NOT a realizable on-chip part) |
| `em5_cideal` | finite candidate | EM 5-turn spiral (5.52 nH) in parallel with an ideal tuning C, in series with R3b |
| `em5_cmim` | finite candidate | EM 5-turn spiral in parallel with the pinned PDK cap_cmim, in series with R3b |
| `em5_cmim_bp10` | bracket | as em5_cmim plus a 10 % bottom-plate parasitic to ground at the base node (ASSUMED bracket: the PDK cap_cmim model carries no bottom-plate capacitance) |
| `em5_cq30` | bracket | as em5_cideal with the tuning C given a series ESR for Q_C = 30 at 2.44175 GHz (bracket for unknown capacitor loss; the PDK cap_cmim ESR is 55 mOhm) |
| `em4_cideal` | finite candidate | EM 4-turn spiral (4.49 nH) in parallel with an ideal tuning C, in series with R3b |
| `em4_cmim` | finite candidate | EM 4-turn spiral in parallel with the pinned PDK cap_cmim, in series with R3b |

Excluded without simulation: `em1`: L = 0.097 nH, Q = 4.1 at 2.44175 GHz: a tank resonant there needs tens of pF (tuning table) and its peak impedance Q*w*L is a few Ohm, far below R3b; it cannot isolate R3b.

Topology: `bref -R3b- b1f -[ XLfeed (EM spiral, DC path) || C ]- b1`. The spiral's
`sub` terminal is the DUT `vss`. Tuning C is chosen so Im(Yser + jwC) = 0 at
2.44175e+09 Hz, where Yser is the MEASURED series-branch admittance of the EM model
(its self-capacitance Cser is inside Yser; the substrate shunts are not part of the
tank and load b1f / b1 in the simulation). Tuning is done once per geometry, not
per-variant optimized.

- Inductors: `sim/models/sg13g2_inductor_em.spice` (stamped from sg13g2-vco; see

  No arbitrary scaled ideal inductor is used for any finite candidate.
- Capacitor: PDK `cap_cmim` (`cornerCAP.lib` section `cap_typ`, `capacitors_mod.lib`),
  sized square by C = 1.5e-15*S^2 + 2*4e-17*(2S) (S in um) and then
  MEASURED as a one-port; the model has a 55 mOhm top-plate resistor and NO
  bottom-plate parasitic capacitance (its header defers that to extraction). That
  evidence is missing and is bracketed (`em5_cmim_bp10`: ASSUMED 10 %), not claimed.
  Generic ideal C with a Q_C = 30 ESR (`em5_cq30`) brackets unknown capacitor loss.
- Transistors / MOS: unchanged `hbt_typ` and `mos_tt` sections; simulator ngspice-46.
- The `ideal_choke` variant is a DIAGNOSTIC BOUND, never a design candidate.

## Policy declared before running (bench policy, NOT ratified)

- DC bias shift flag: |IC1 / IC1(committed) - 1| or the same for IDD > 1%.
- Operating-point validity flags: VCE < 0.3 V or VBC > 0.4 V on Q1/Q2;
  non-positive collector current; any non-normal ngspice convergence (singular
  matrix, failed gmin/source stepping, transient-op fallback). Flags are reported;
  the bias is never retuned to hide them.
- Best valid feed: lowest worst-in-band NFmin@290 among `finite candidate` variants
  with no DC flag and normal convergence (brackets are reported, not selected).
- Matching verification: the existing `lp_noise` noise-weighted input match,
  Q = 10 case, re-synthesized from each feed's own characterization; DC reference
  `Rxoutdc = 1e+11` Ohm on xout (the #190 convention, R-independent 1e9..1e11).
  The committed feed and the ideal choke are matched the same way for comparison.
- Go/no-go for a later full-PVT campaign: GO only if, for the best valid feed, the
  VERIFIED matched worst-in-band NF@290 <= 1.5 - 0.3 dB (an allowance
  for process/temperature/supply/passive spread that a nominal cell cannot measure),
  S11 and S22 < -10 dB, |S21| > 15 dB across the band, no resolved mu < 1 over
  10 MHz-30 GHz, DC-valid, convergent. Otherwise NO-GO, and the first failing gate in
  the order dc_validity > convergence > noise_floor > noise_match_penalty > noise_margin > s11 > s22 > s21 > stability is reported as the binding constraint.

## Run budget

Single-process `ngspice -b` decks, strictly sequential, nominal cell only: 1 tuning,
1 capacitor probe, 8 feed scans, 3 characterizations, 3 verifications = 16. No
corner grid, no Monte Carlo, no fleet submission (and no local-grid fallback).
