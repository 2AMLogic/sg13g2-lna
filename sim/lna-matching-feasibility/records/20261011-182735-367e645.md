## What was wrong in `20261011-164702-455c67d`

- The reducer iterated both arms but always read the finite `em5_cmim` tank probe, so the fresh
  ideal-choke control row was given that tank's isolation (797.9 Ohm mid / 733.0 Ohm band-min).
  The ideal 1 uH choke is a different feed circuit; it has no finite-tank measurement. The pairing
  "797.9 Ohm with NFmin@290 worst 0.476 dB" is unsupported (the physical finite feed at that
  isolation has 1.545 dB).
- The reducer then appended a second `ideal_choke` row from the retained #198 characterization
  (same numbers, isolation "infinite"), so the table held two indistinguishable `ideal_choke`
  rows. They are the same NFmin values from two sources (fresh #200 control char vs retained
  #198 anchor), which is why the values coincide; they are not independent points.

## Correction

- Only the finite-feed control (`em5_cmim`) and the three bracket proxies carry the measured
  tank isolation and form the measured finite isolation curve.
- The ideal-choke rows are diagnostic bounds with isolation NOT MEASURED (the ideal 1 uH choke is
  a bound; this record makes no claim about its actual impedance). They are named
  `ideal_choke_ctl_fresh` and `ideal_choke_retained_anchor`, carry distinct `source` values and
  are excluded from the finite curve (`finite_curve_member = no`).
- Unchanged and consistent with the raw inputs: all finite-feed values, the input-Q map
  (re-derived qmap table is byte-identical to `20261011-164702-455c67d-qmap.csv`), the boundary verifications,
  the threshold crossings and the campaign verdict (NO-GO, binding: noise_floor). No spec change.
